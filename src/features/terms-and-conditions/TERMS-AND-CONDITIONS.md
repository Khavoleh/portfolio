# Terms & Conditions

## Purpose

The legal terms page for the portfolio site. Tells visitors the site is a personal portfolio (not a service offer), what they may/may not do with its content and code snippets, and where liability sits. Composed of a heading, a last-updated stamp, and six ordered legal sections (acceptance, intellectual property, disclaimers, governing law, external links, contact), rendered once per language. Read by visitors who want the legal fine print before reusing code or content from the site.
